Cookie Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

In short. Our own pages use only small items of browser storage and no tracking cookies. Signing in uses Google’s sign-in buttons, which Google provides and which may set Google’s own cookies.

1. What we store on your device

NameCategoryPurposeHow long
besta_consent_v2Strictly necessaryRemembers your cookie choice so we do not ask again on every visit.Until you clear your browser data or change your choice
besta_dasara_highscoreFunctional (only if you allow it)Remembers your best Dasara Arcade score on this device. It holds a number only.Until you clear your browser data or turn this off

Your sign-in session is kept only in the page’s memory. It is not stored in cookies or local storage and disappears when you log out, after a period of inactivity, or when you close the page.

2. Google sign-in (third party)

To let you sign up and log in with your Google account, the home page loads Google’s sign-in buttons from Google. Google may set its own cookies or store data in your browser under the Google Privacy Policy. This is part of the sign-in service itself, so it is not one of the choices you can switch off here.

3. What we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, analytics or tracking pixels, and we do not load web fonts or other resources from third parties.

4. Change your choice

You can change your choice at any time with the buttons below, or by choosing “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the kiosk. You can also delete stored items in your browser settings.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@besta.co.in. See also our Privacy Policy.
