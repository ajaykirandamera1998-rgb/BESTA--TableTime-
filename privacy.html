Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

In short. You sign up once with your Google account and log in on later visits. We receive your name and email from Google. If you choose, you can also share a mobile number to receive coupons and offers. We never ask for passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar or PAN. Your selfie never leaves your device. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

This policy explains how Besta — The Indian Kitchen handles personal data collected through the games on this website and kiosk (Table Time and Dasara Arcade). It is written to follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”).

1. Who we are

The Data Fiduciary is Besta — The Indian Kitchen, Beside DBS Bank, Kanaka Durga Gazetted Officers Colony, Guru Nanak Colony, Vijayawada. You can reach us at hello@besta.co.in.

We collect only what is needed for the purposes below.

3. What we do not collect

  • We never ask for a password, a one-time password (OTP), Aadhaar, PAN or any other government ID number.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, payment details, or any biometric data.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not build profiles of you.

4. Your photo (Table Time)

When you choose to take or pick a photo, the browser on your device turns it into the puzzle. The photo is not uploaded, not sent to us and not saved. It is erased from the device’s memory when you leave the game, sign out, or after a period of inactivity. The camera is used only after you press the camera button and allow it in your browser.

5. Consent, and taking it back

We process your data only with your consent, which you give by ticking the boxes shown before you sign in and, separately, before you share a mobile number. Nothing is pre-ticked. The mobile number and offers are optional, and playing does not depend on them.

You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: choose Delete my data in the app, or email hello@besta.co.in. Withdrawing consent for offers stops our offers messages. Withdrawing the sign-in consent means we delete your data and you will no longer appear on the leaderboard.

6. Children (under 18)

Signing up, logging in and the offers feature are for people aged 18 or above. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18, we do not track or monitor children’s behaviour, and we do not direct advertising at them. If you believe a child’s data has been given to us, email us and we will delete it.

7. Who we share data with

  • Google provides Google Sign-In and, through Google Sheets and Google Apps Script, the place where your data is stored. When the home page opens, your browser contacts Google to load the sign-in buttons, so Google receives technical data such as your IP address and may set cookies under its own policy.
  • Our website host delivers the pages and processes the technical data described above.
  • If we send offers using a messaging service, that provider acts only on our instructions.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

8. Where your data is stored

Data is stored in a private Google Sheet that only Besta can open. Google and our host may process data on servers outside India. We transfer data only to places that Indian law allows.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use the games and delete it automatically 12 months after your last visit, or sooner if you ask. Records we must keep by law are kept only for as long as the law requires.

10. How we protect it

We take reasonable security steps: the site uses HTTPS; the sign-in is checked with Google on our server; the spreadsheet is private; requests are limited and checked before anything is stored; and web addresses and keys that must stay private are kept out of the page. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

11. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • have your data corrected, completed or updated;
  • have your data erased;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • have your grievance addressed; and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or cannot do so yourself.

To use any of these rights, email hello@besta.co.in from the address you signed in with, or use Delete my data inside the app for immediate deletion. We will reply within 30 days.

12. Complaints

Please contact us first at hello@besta.co.in so we can put things right. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, where the change matters, ask for your consent again. See also our Terms of Service and Cookie Policy.
